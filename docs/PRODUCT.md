# Product brief

## Problem

Small breaks are easy to spend scrolling even when there is a strong desire to learn. Selecting content across apps creates friction, and consumption alone does not establish understanding. Interlude offers a useful next lesson and connects short sessions over time.

## Intended user and context

One private owner. An iPhone is the everyday reading/listening device; a laptop supports reading, course management, and reflection. Typical sessions are around five minutes. Listening should work during chores, walking, and waiting, with lock-screen and headphone controls. Online connectivity is assumed.

## Learning loop

1. Open Today and resume the current lesson or start the next one.
2. Read or play the same core teaching.
3. Optionally answer a recall question, reflect, or try a practical challenge.
4. Stop at a clear boundary, with progress saved.
5. Revisit a useful idea or choose a bounded discovery later.

A longer session uses a consciously selected queue of at most three lessons. Optional source links and embeds supplement the self-contained core lesson. No core lesson requires switching to another app.

## Content

Each original lesson teaches one objective through approximately 600–800 spoken words, a concrete example, a takeaway, and relevant sources. Reading and listening refer to the same lesson version. Source dates and version history remain visible. Revisions preserve the old text, audio, progress, and reflections.

The starter paths are AI product management, AI software development, and AI in pharma/healthcare. Standalone discovery can extend beyond those paths. The system supports reviewed source-based generation, but a generated draft must pass the evidence and release checks before it becomes a lesson.

## What success would look like

Less self-reported scrolling, a regular learning habit, completed courses, ideas remembered later, and concrete applications at work or home. These are intended outcomes, not measured results. The app does not inspect other apps or automatically prove a reduction in screen time.

A contribution-style calendar makes activity visible without streak pressure. Completion, recall, and application remain separate measures. A page visit is not automatically counted as meaningful learning.

## Constraints and non-goals

- One owner and a small recurring budget, targeted below $10/month.
- Durable progress across devices and playback at a default 1.5× speed.
- Core learning remains inside the app; optional media is explicitly loaded.
- No infinite feed, engagement-maximizing autoplay, or penalty for stopping.
- No offline downloads, social network, classroom administration, subscription marketplace, or Audible account integration in the current scope.

The most important unresolved questions concern long-term learning usefulness, AI answer quality, interruption recovery, device compatibility, and actual provider costs. Feature completion alone does not answer them.
