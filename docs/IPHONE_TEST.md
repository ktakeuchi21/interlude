# iPhone playback checklist

Use a private HTTPS deployment with at least two saved narrations. Record the iPhone, iOS, headphone model, and whether the test uses Safari or the installed home-screen app. Run against your own test account/deployment; these actions save progress.

1. Sign in and start a lesson on the speaker. Confirm the default 1.5× speed, change speed, and seek forward/backward.
2. Play a complete lesson with the phone locked. Pause and resume using lock-screen controls.
3. Connect AirPods and test pause/resume. Interrupt playback with a call or Siri, then resume and check the saved position.
4. Open the app on the laptop. Confirm progress and a saved reflection. Change the reading section and verify that it does not reset audio progress on the phone.
5. Add the app to the home screen from Safari and repeat the speaker, locked-screen and headphone checks.
6. Select two lessons in the listening queue. Lock the phone before the first finishes; verify that the second starts with sound and updated controls, then playback stops at the queue's end.
7. Navigate away while audio plays. Tap the footer player and confirm the correct lesson opens without losing playback or the queue.
8. Quit and reopen the installed app and verify the saved lesson and position.

Record pass/fail and the first unexpected behavior. Keep physical-device results separate from desktop or automated tests. Recheck after changes to the player, media format, hosting headers or target iOS version.
