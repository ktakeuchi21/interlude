# Roadmap

## Implemented

- Three eight-lesson foundation courses with citations and optional practice.
- A current/next-lesson home card, reading, saved narration and a persistent player.
- Durable progress, activity calendar, course controls, notes and exports.
- Bounded questions, research, source capture, reviewed drafting and versioned release.
- Short spoken input with editable transcription.
- Separate API, narration and storage accounting with conservative recovery.
- Trusted plan-based weekly publications and a documented personal scheduling workflow.

## Next work

1. Complete safe, explicit replacement of missing Chirp segments while retaining original usage; improve resilience to long-request interruptions.
2. Finish live narration preparation for the remaining foundation lessons in the private deployment.
3. Evaluate answer grounding, generated-lesson teaching quality and transcription using realistic personal tasks.
4. Repeat targeted iPhone/AirPods and media-supplement checks where coverage remains uncertain.
5. Compare actual provider invoices and storage growth with the application's estimates over time.
6. Assess the learning habit after real use: what was remembered, applied, and useful enough to revisit.

## Possible later directions

More subject areas, better spaced recall and a more portable authentication/deployment adapter. These are possibilities, not committed releases. Offline audio, multi-user accounts and commercial subscription features are not current priorities.
